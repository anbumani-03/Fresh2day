# 🛒 Fresh2Day

### 🥦 Fresh Groceries & Food Products at Your Fingertips

Fresh2Day is a modern online grocery and food shopping website designed to make everyday shopping simple and convenient.

🌐 **Live Website:** [Fresh2Day](https://fresh2-day.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🥬 Fresh Fruits & Vegetables
- 🍚 Rice & Dals
- 🌾 Atta & Flours
- 🌶️ Masala & Spices
- 🫒 Oils & Ghee
- 🥛 Dairy & Eggs
- ☕ Tea, Coffee & Health Drinks
- 🥜 Dry Fruits
- 🍪 Cookies & Biscuits
- 🍫 Chocolates & Sweets
- 🥤 Drinks & Juices
- 🍿 Snacks & Chips
- 🏠 Household Essentials
- 💄 Beauty & Personal Care
- 🎂 Cakes & Ice Cream
- 🎁 Gifting Zone

---

## 🛍️ Shopping Features

- 🔎 Product search
- 📂 Browse products by category
- 🛒 Product selection
- 💰 Product pricing
- 🔐 User login
- 📍 Location selection
- 🎉 Special offers and discounted products

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Fresh2Day aims to provide a convenient digital shopping experience where users can explore different grocery and food categories and easily find products for their everyday needs.
